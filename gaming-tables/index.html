Customizations

My gaming tables are all custom designed and made to order, so the sky is the limit in terms of customization. Here's a list of some common customizations I make for my clients:

  • Overall dimensions, including height and size of the play area
  • Custom table shape (ex. hexagonal)
  • Play area material (felt, neoprene, solid wood, etc)
  • Various aesthetic details, such as the shape of the legs or beveled edges
  • Custom inlay details
